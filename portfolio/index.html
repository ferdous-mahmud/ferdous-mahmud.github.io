About

I'm an iOS engineer who cares about the details that make apps feel effortless — smooth scrolling, instant search, downloads that survive a lost connection. Over the past 2+ years I've shipped production apps used across Saudi Arabia and Bangladesh, spanning ride-sharing, social media, and enterprise document management.

Outside of shipping features, I've published 50+ iOS articles read by 3000+ developers monthly, solved 250+ algorithmic problems, and mentored junior engineers on architecture and code quality.
